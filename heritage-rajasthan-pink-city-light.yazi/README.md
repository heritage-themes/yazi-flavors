# Rajasthan Pink City (Light) for yazi

Rajasthan Pink City (Light) is a light flavor of [Heritage Themes](https://github.com/heritage-themes/heritage-themes) for [yazi](https://github.com/sxyazi/yazi), from the Rajasthan family: The colours of Rajasthan's cities and cloth: kesariya-saffron keywords, rani-pink strings, golden-sandstone types and blue-pottery methods, on the blue houses of Jodhpur by night or the pink sandstone of Jaipur by day. Its colours are those of the Rajasthan Pink City (Light) theme of the [Heritage Themes for terminals](https://github.com/heritage-themes/heritage-themes/releases/latest), which the terminal should run with it.

With this folder in yazi's `flavors` folder, name the flavor in yazi's `theme.toml`:

```toml
[flavor]
light = "heritage-rajasthan-pink-city-light"
```

The [family's page](https://github.com/heritage-themes/heritage-themes/tree/master/themes/rajasthan) describes its colour tradition.
